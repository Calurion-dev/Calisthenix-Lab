About · The Lab

A lab, not a coach.

Calisthenix-Lab explains calisthenics without selling a programme or promising results. The goal is simple: that you understand what you are doing, and start cleanly.

The intention

Calisthenics is usually presented in one of two ways: either as an inaccessible practice reserved for spectacular skills, or as a series of Motivation™ videos with no explanation. Between the two, a clear entry point is missing.

Calisthenix-Lab wants to be that entry point. Not a personal blog, not a tracking app, not a shop. An educational site: simple definitions, six movements explained properly, and progression that deliberately moves from the fundamentals to the skills.

The name says it. “Lab” for the approach: observe, break down, verify. No miracle recipe, no empty superlatives. Movements, execution criteria, and permission to start small.

What this site is not

  • Not a personalised programme: no prescription adapted to your level, age or history.
  • Not tracking: no account, no training data, no coach behind the screen.
  • Not a medical source: the content is educational, not diagnostic.
  • Not a commercial space: no affiliations, no products to sell.

Health

Calisthenix-Lab provides general information for educational purposes. The content is not medical advice, a diagnosis or a prescription, and does not replace the guidance of a healthcare professional or a qualified coach.

Before starting any training, consult a doctor if you have a medical condition, an injury, persistent pain or any doubt. Stop immediately if you feel joint pain, unusual shortness of breath or dizziness, and seek advice.

The line not to crossMuscle soreness that fades within a few days is normal. Sharp pain that persists or wakes you at night is not. When in doubt, you stop.
