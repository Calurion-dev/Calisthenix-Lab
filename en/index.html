Your body is the only machine you need.

Calisthenics is training with your own bodyweight. No gym, no machines: five well-executed movements are enough to start — and to keep progressing for years.

What exactly is it?

Three answers, three variants.

Definition

Training by moving your own bodyweight. Your body is the load, gravity is the machine. Calisthenics is not a trend: it is the oldest way to train.

Where?

At home, in a park, on a street workout area. A floor is enough to start. A high bar, two stable supports and a little space open up the rest.

For whom?

Beginners and advanced practitioners alike. Every movement has an easier version and a harder one. You pick the one you can execute cleanly.

Variants

Three ways to organise the same training, depending on the goal. RIR (reps in reserve): the reps you had left before failure.

Strength

High intensity, low volume. Stay between 0 and 3 RIR, over 3 to 5 sets, with 3 to 5 minutes of rest.

Memory hookThe 3×5: 3 to 5 reps, 3 to 5 sets, 3 to 5 minutes of rest.

Endurance

The opposite: high volume, low intensity. You chain sets with little rest.

Hypertrophy

The method for building muscle. You must be very close to failure (1 to 2 RIR maximum). Your body undergoes intense stress and rebuilds its fibres bigger to resist next time.

The method

Three levels, in this order.

Progression is not a trend: each level assumes the previous one. That is what makes the skills possible without wrecking your shoulders along the way.

  1. 01

    Fundamentals

    Push, pull, bend your legs, brace your trunk. Five complete movements, executed cleanly, before anything else.

    Available
  2. 02

    Static

    Hold a position without moving: L-sit, planche, front lever. Strength, bracing and patience.

    V2 — coming
  3. 03

    Dynamic

    Add momentum: muscle-up, handstand, transitions. The playground, once the foundations are solid.

    V3 — coming

Why start

What changes day to day.

Useful strength

Not show muscle: a back that holds, stable shoulders, a trunk that transfers force between your arms and legs.

Freedom

A park, a bedroom, a playground. Training adapts to where you are, not the other way around.

Minimal equipment

The floor, a bar, maybe two supports. No subscription, no machine to adjust.

Measurable progress

Reps, seconds held, skills unlocked: progress is visible, even in weeks when motivation drops.

Starting point

Start with five movements.
